About this model

Dataset

Trained on the popular 1025-row "Heart Disease Dataset" mirror of the UCI Cleveland Heart Disease data (13 clinical features + target). This file is the original ~303-patient Cleveland dataset with rows duplicated to pad it out — there are only about 302 distinct patient records in it.

To avoid inflated accuracy from duplicate rows leaking between training and test data, the train/test split (and cross-validation folds) were done at the patient-record level: every duplicate copy of the same record is guaranteed to stay on the same side of the split. See train_model.py in the repository for the exact methodology.

Limitations

  • The dataset has ~302 unique patient records — small by modern ML standards, so accuracy estimates have real uncertainty.
  • The data comes from a specific 1980s-era Cleveland Clinic patient population and may not generalize to other populations.
  • This app is for learning/demonstration purposes only and is not a validated clinical or diagnostic tool.
